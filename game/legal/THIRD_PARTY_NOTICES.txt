Third-party notices

Third-party creators retain their rights in their respective works. The project permission for free play applies to the team's original material and preserves the permissions granted by each third-party license.

The individual works, creators, sources and known licenses follow below. Full CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0, CC0 1.0 and Inter OFL texts are included in this directory and the in-game license selector. Godot's engine and compiled third-party component notices are available through the same Licenses menu.

Game adaptations include scene scale, materials, placement and composition. The cat and dog additionally use revised coats and animations. The VHS shader was adapted for Godot 4, capture timing and the game's visual settings. Audio is mixed in gameplay, and selected clips are looped or cropped. The included fonts and default Godot icon retain their supplied artwork.
