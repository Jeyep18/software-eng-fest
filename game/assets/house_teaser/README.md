# Approved house teaser set

Recording-only house states: Normal reuses the complete authored gameplay house, and Wrecked uses the visually approved Blender damage/flood model with Godot's additional props. Blender source: `C:/Users/john2/Documents/Blender/Bagyong_Bahay_House_Teaser/house_teaser_states.blend`. The original `house_inside1.blend` and the approved review file remain unchanged.

In a developer run, open **Pause → Dev Mode → Recording Camera**, select **House teaser**, choose **Normal** or **Wrecked**, and enter camera mode. **G** switches the two states without changing the shot. Escape restores the original gameplay scene and pause panel. Reset Camera returns to the approved living/dining composition; other angles use the ordinary flying controls. Recording location and state preferences last only for this application session.

The two GLBs preserve source geometry, scale, and shared origin, with Blender Z-up converted to glTF/Godot Y-up and embedded textures extracted during native import. At runtime, the Normal GLB supplies its review cameras; its meshes are replaced by a visual copy of `house_inside.tscn`. The Wrecked GLB and review cameras use the gameplay model's authored transform. Native doors, TV, fridge, sofa, chairs, eleven trees and surroundings retain their gameplay placement and scale. Wrecked retains the additional props and surroundings but omits the two door leaves and the exported duplicate surroundings. Both states remain loaded during a take.

Normal includes Nanay and Lola visual copies that obey **Hide NPCs**; Wrecked retains the approved empty-house composition. The TV moves with its displaced cabinet, while Wrecked keeps its approved damaged sofa without an overlapping intact copy. Visual copies are stripped of gameplay scripts, groups, UI, audio, player/spawn logic and collision participation before entering the tree. They do not run conversations or change session progress.

`export_house_states.py` is the reproducible Blender MCP export step. It evaluates modifiers/curves into static meshes in temporary scenes and converts legacy diffuse materials to glTF-compatible PBR nodes. Wet color multipliers are preserved. It restores the Blender scene without saving. The packed-image encoder fix uses explicit copy paths and does not edit Blender's installed exporter. `export_manifest.json` records the exports.

The runtime stage is `game/scenes/recording/house_teaser.tscn`. Normal uses the authored house environment, practical lamps and GI. Wrecked uses the existing storm outdoor HDRI/fog, cool practical lights and full-intensity house rain independent of the gameplay clock. Both use the source directional light and the ordinary recording environment helper. State changes rebuild the helper from the selected state's environment so exposure does not accumulate; Sunrise/Sunny/Sunset replace the sky and mute rain as in ordinary locations. Rain inherits world freeze, with preprocessed particles visible when a frozen take starts. Original gameplay meshes, lights, GI and processing are suppressed and restored on exit. The set has no fluid simulation or gameplay travel registration.

Verification and in-game comparisons are recorded in `docs/TESTING.md` and `design_previews/house_teaser/`. The source assets were supplied/approved by the user; approval does not add license claims for their underlying textures.
